03 Information We Collect
3.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

Olah Skill Sejarah Dunia is designed with a privacy-first approach. We explicitly do NOT collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information
  • User accounts — no registration or login required
  • GPS location data or geographic coordinates
  • Financial information, credit card details, or payment data
  • Contact lists, messages, or personal communications
  • Photos, videos, or media files from your device
  • Biometric data (fingerprints, facial recognition)
  • Social media account data
  • Audio or video recordings
3.2 Minimal Technical Data Automatically Collected

Some minimal technical data may be automatically collected by the Android system or Google Play Services:

  • Device OS type and version (e.g., Android 12) for compatibility
  • Device model (e.g., Samsung Galaxy A) for display compatibility
  • Anonymous crash reports for technical troubleshooting
  • Anonymous, aggregated app performance data

This data is anonymous, cannot identify you personally, and is only used to improve the application.

04 Data Stored on Your Device

To provide a good gameplay experience, the app stores some data locally on your device:

  • High scores and game history
  • Level or stage progression
  • User preference settings (volume, language, theme)
  • Personal game statistics (correct/incorrect answers)

All local data resides entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties. You can remove it by clearing the app cache or uninstalling the app.

05 How We Use Information

Minimal technical data (such as crash reports) is used solely to:

  • Detect and fix technical bugs or errors
  • Improve app performance and stability
  • Ensure compatibility across various Android devices
  • Analyze error patterns for future development

We do not use any data for marketing, targeted advertising, or sale to third parties.
